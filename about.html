About Christos Media

Christos Media was established in November 2012 as a non-denominational Christian organization, built up by God to influence Ghana and the world through media and other platforms as the Lord directs.

Over the years, as we've carried the Good News of our Lord Jesus Christ into schools, we've seen a spiritual hunger in people that nothing material can quench — only the Lord Himself can. As the curtain of time draws close to an end, we feel the urgency of sending the gospel deliberately, and forcefully, across the world.

As you consider partnering with Christos Media, our desire is simply to help you fulfil God's master plan — because every believer is an active, committed part of it. We invite you to prayerfully consider partnership.
