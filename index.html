About Christos Media

Over the years, as we've carried the Good News of our Lord Jesus Christ into schools, we've seen a spiritual hunger in people that nothing material can quench — only the Lord Himself can. As time draws closer to its end, we feel the need to send the gospel deliberately, and forcefully, across the world.

As you consider partnering with Christos Media, our desire is simply to help you fulfil God's master plan — because every believer is called to be an active, committed part of it. We'd love to help you seek the Lord for your place in His agenda through Christos. Take a look at what's here, and ask God if this is where He'd have you partner the gospel.

Christos Media was established in November 2012 as a non-denominational Christian organization, built up by God to influence this nation and the world at large through media and every other platform He directs.

Our Role

What we're here to do

In a world of chaos, Christos Media stands as a source of hope — probing this generation into its God-given purpose, one honest conversation at a time.

A Source of Hope

We give this generation news of faith, hope and love — words that point people back to their God-given purpose and abilities, seen from a higher perspective.

Shows Their Worth & Responsibility

Beyond positive talks, we build the wisdom and mindset of the just through the ministry of the Word and the Holy Spirit.

A Medium for Winning Souls

Through the Holy Spirit, we present the gospel simply yet powerfully — not just a change of mind, but a transformation of heart.

Identifies & Helps Solve Critical Issues

Part of our social responsibility is offering godly counsel on critical issues facing the nation and Africa, and — as much as God allows — helping the needy in our communities and beyond.

A Light in the Media Industry

Beyond our role as a media company to the world, we aim to be a light to others working within the industry itself.

“We are a source of hope to this generation.”

A team of committed volunteers carrying the gospel into schools, churches, hospitals, prisons and streets across Ghana.

How We Make a Difference

Conferences, outreach & compassion

Five ways Christos Media puts the gospel to work — in classrooms, in leadership, and on the streets.

StartRight Conference

An empowerment summit bringing together JHS, SHS and tertiary students to grow in academics, relationships, career choices and spiritual life.

Leadership Retreats

Critical sessions equipping young men and women already in leadership roles to lead with greater effectiveness.

Gospel Outreaches

The message of salvation carried into basic, secondary and tertiary institutions. In 2023 alone, four senior high schools saw 1,286 souls won.

Compassion & Charity

Following Christ's call to feed the hungry, visit the sick and visit those in prison, we've spent five years reaching orphanages, hospitals, prisons and the streets.

Scholarships

With the help of our partners, brilliant but needy students receive scholarships to continue their education at the SHS and tertiary level.
