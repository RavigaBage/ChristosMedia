How We Make a Difference

Conferences, outreach & compassion

Five ways Christos Media puts the gospel to work — in classrooms, in leadership, and on the streets.

StartRight Conference

An empowerment summit bringing together JHS, SHS and tertiary students to grow in academics, relationships, career choices and spiritual life.

Leadership Retreats

Critical sessions equipping young men and women already in leadership roles to lead with greater effectiveness.

Gospel Outreaches

The message of salvation carried into basic, secondary and tertiary institutions. In 2023 alone, four senior high schools saw 1,286 souls won.

Compassion & Charity

Following Christ's call to feed the hungry, visit the sick and visit those in prison, we've spent five years reaching orphanages, hospitals, prisons and the streets.

Scholarships

With the help of our partners, brilliant but needy students receive scholarships to continue their education at the SHS and tertiary level.
